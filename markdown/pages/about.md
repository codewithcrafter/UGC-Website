# We Find the Right Creator for Your Product.

MY UGC VIDEO is a specialized User Generated Content (UGC) video production studio based in Delhi NCR, India. We connect direct-to-consumer (D2C) companies, e-commerce retailers, and high-growth brands with everyday content creators who produce honest, relatable short-form video content.

We bridge the gap between polished traditional commercial advertising and raw social media authenticity — giving brands content that builds undeniable trust and lowers customer acquisition costs.

---

## Why We Started This

Traditional studio advertising is expensive, slow, and increasingly ignored by modern digital consumers. Today's buyers scroll past glossy corporate commercials and seek recommendations from relatable peers on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube.

### For Brands
Brands struggled to locate reliable creators, negotiate fair usage contracts, manage shipping logistics, and enforce high-converting direct-response scripting. We created an end-to-end studio management layer that makes commissioning creator content as simple as ordering software.

### For Creators
Talented short-form creators often spent excessive time pitching brands and chasing invoices rather than creating great videos. We provide clear creative briefs, competitive compensation, and reliable project workflows that respect their creative energy.

## Where We'd Like This to Go

Our studio vision centers on three pillars:

- **Longer Creator-Brand Relationships:** Moving away from one-off transactional posts toward consistent monthly creative iteration where creators become genuine brand ambassadors.
- **Accessibility for Any Brand Size:** Providing enterprise-grade creative strategy and vetted creator casting whether you are an emerging bootstrap DTC startup or an established household brand.
- **New Creative Formats:** Continually experimenting with emerging vertical video styles, kinetic subtitling, multi-angle unboxings, and AI-assisted variation testing.

## How a Project Runs: Our 6-Step Workflow

1. **Send Us the Brief:** Share your product details, customer profile, key value propositions, and campaign goals through our structured onboarding form.
2. **We Pick the Creators:** We match your brand with creators whose demographics, aesthetic style, and everyday lifestyle align naturally with your ideal customer profile.
3. **Scripts & Hooks Development:** We draft 3-second hook variations and narrative story arcs designed to maximize video completion rate and click-through rate.
4. **Creators Film:** The creator receives the product sample and films high-resolution footage in authentic, natural environments.
5. **Edit & Quality Check:** Our in-house post-production team edits the footage, integrates subtitles, color-grades, and mixes licensed sound effects.
6. **You Get the Files:** Receive final high-resolution MP4 video assets ready for immediate upload to Meta Ads Manager, TikTok Ads, YouTube Shorts, or your Shopify store.
