_FAQS_

## Common Questions

Short answers on rights, creators, timelines and files.

### What commercial usage rights do you provide?

You can use the finished videos in paid ads on Meta, TikTok, YouTube and Google. The exact platforms and duration are in your project agreement, so read that before signing.

### How long does production take?

Most projects take about 7 to 12 business days, from brief to final video. A big batch or slow feedback can add time.

### How do you select creators?

We look at your product, your audience and the platform. Then we shortlist creators whose style and followers suit it, and who are free on your dates.

### Do clients approve scripts before filming?

Yes. We send the script and hooks first. Filming starts only after you say yes.

### What aspect ratios are delivered?

9:16, 4:5, 1:1 and 16:9, as MP4 files. Captions can be burned in, and we can send separate SRT subtitle files too.

### How are revisions handled?

Send all your feedback together and we'll make the changes.
