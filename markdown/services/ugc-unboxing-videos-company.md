## Why Unboxing Videos Make People Want to Buy

Ads that look like TV commercials get skipped. A video that feels like it came from a real person gets
watched, and that's often when someone stops scrolling and starts thinking about buying.

Opening the box is the first time a customer actually touches your brand. Online, nobody can walk into a
store, so watching somebody else open a nicely packed order is the next best thing.

As a UGC unboxing video agency, we follow the whole moment: cutting the tape, lifting the lid, finding
what's inside, feeling the materials and reacting on the spot. Buyers see that the packaging is good
quality, and they want the same parcel on their doorstep. That's why unboxing videos for e-commerce brands
keep turning up in feeds and on product pages.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do UGC unboxing videos work so well for e-commerce brands?

Buyers can't hold the product before they order. Watching someone else open it is the next best thing,
and it shows them what will land at their door.

### Should we send special PR packaging or standard customer packaging?

Standard customer packaging is usually better, because it shows buyers what they'll actually receive. If
you'd like PR packaging, say so in the brief.

### Do you capture audio for ASMR-style unboxings?

Yes. Clear audio is part of every shoot, and ASMR is one of our formats.

### Can creators demonstrate the product after unboxing it?

Yes. Add it to the brief and we'll write it into the script for you to approve.

### How long does an unboxing package take?

Usually 7 to 12 business days. A big package or slow feedback can add some time.
