_Overview_

## Why Brands Use *UGC Video Ads* for Paid Social

People skip ads that look like ads. They stop for something that looks like a friend's video.

A polished studio commercial often feels out of place on a phone. A creator-made ad looks like it belongs in the feed, so people stay a little longer. Those extra seconds are all an ad really gets, which is why direct-response UGC ads work so well for performance video ads.

Each ad is built around one clear idea. It could be a problem your buyer already feels, a close look at the product, a price that feels like good value, or a customer saying it worked for them. Then we write a few different openings for each idea, so your media buyer can run them side by side and see which one holds attention.

### A Strong First Few Seconds

Viewers decide fast, usually before the first few seconds are over. So we spend the most time on thumb-stopping hooks and treat the opening as the most important part of the ad.

### Start Strong

If the start is weak, the rest never gets watched. That’s why we fuss over the first line more than anything else in the video.

_FAQs_

## Questions People Ask

### How do UGC video ads differ from regular organic UGC?

Organic UGC is content you post like normal. An ad is made for paid media, so it needs a strong start, one clear message, and something that tells people what to do next. We also make a few versions so you can test which one works.

### How many hook variations do you provide with each ad?

Usually a few per video. Want more? Put it in your brief and we’ll send you a quote.

### Do you design ads for Meta and YouTube specifically?

Yes. We set the size, captions and text position for each platform. What works on Meta doesn’t always work on YouTube Shorts.

### Can we run these videos as Spark Ads or whitelisted creator ads?

It depends on the creator saying yes and on what your agreement covers.

### What is the recommended turnaround time for new ad packs?

Usually 7 to 12 business days. If the pack is big or feedback comes late, UGC video ad turnaround can take longer.
