_OVERVIEW_

## A Quick Word on UGC

UGC means user-generated content. For us it's simply a video that looks like a person made it, because one did.

People skip ads that look like ads. They'll watch a video that feels like a friend telling them about something they liked.

That's why brands choose creator-led video content, and why many of them look for UGC video creation services.

But good UGC content creation doesn't happen by accident. If you just post a product to a creator and wait, you could end up with a nice video that never says what's different about yours. So we plan first. The creator still speaks in their own words, but they know what to cover.

### Casting

Follower count matters less to us. What matters is whether your customer would actually listen to this person.

### Openings

Every video comes with a few different first lines. These hook variations won’t all perform the same, and you’ll see which ones work better.
