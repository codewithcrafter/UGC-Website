_FAQs_

## Common Questions

### Does AI UGC video creation look natural to social media users?

Mostly, yes. We put the AI presenter over real footage of your product, and that’s a big part of why it feels believable. It won't suit every brand though, so we’d suggest testing AI UGC vs real creator UGC side by side.

### Can we translate our existing winning UGC videos into other languages?

Yes. We can re-voice and lip-sync a video that’s already working. Right now that means Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and English.

### How quickly can you deliver an AI UGC video batch?

New versions can be ready in 48 to 72 hours. A bigger batch will take a bit longer, and we’ll share the AI UGC video turnaround dates when you send the brief.

### Are AI personas cleared for commercial advertising?

You get commercial ad rights with every video, and the details are in your agreement. Please read it before you sign.

### Which products suit AI UGC videos best?

Products that are easy to show up close and easy to explain in 15 to 45 seconds. If your product needs a long demo or a lot of explaining, a real creator video usually works better.

### Do I need to label AI-made ads on Meta and TikTok?

Platform rules on AI labels can change, so check the latest policy before you upload. We’ll flag it in your brief and help you set it up.
