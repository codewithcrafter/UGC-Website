## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which products are best suited to UGC product videos?

Any products that your customers would want to see before purchasing. Beauty, Skincare, Kitchenware,
Homeware & Clothing all do well as they have texture and finish.

### Is this suitable for all products?

No. If your product requires a long technical video or customers don't care about it looking nice up
close then a creator video won't help much. We'll let you know before you pay.

### Can we request certain features to be shown?

Yes! We'll include these in your brief. They will be added to your script and shot list. You'll approve
these.

### Will we receive raw footage?

Yes! We'll include this if requested. Just mention this in your brief.

### Can we use these on our Shopify product page?

Yes! They're regular MP4s so you can upload them.

### How many samples should we send?

Typically one per creator. If your product runs out whilst filming then send an extra.

### How long will it take?

Usually 7-12 working days from brief to final files. If samples arrive late or we have slow feedback
this can be longer.
