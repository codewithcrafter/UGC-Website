_Overview_

## Why People Trust Videos

Images are staged and everyone knows this, including your customers. By showing a regular person using your product at their own kitchen counter, it feels more authentic.

This is what our UGC product videos are based on. A real person that looks and sounds like your customer. No shiny background and no script. We think this is the whole point of being a UGC Product Video Agency.

Imagine a face serum being applied to the back of their hand or a blender lid locking into place. In just a few seconds it answers all your questions that your product page can't. This is why UGC video for e-commerce has become popular. [ADD: one genuine example from a previous project]

_FAQs_

## Frequently Asked *Questions*

### Which products are best suited to UGC product videos?

Any products that your customers would want to see before purchasing. Beauty, Skincare, Kitchenware, Homeware & Clothing all do well as they have texture and finish.

### Is this suitable for all products?

No. If your product requires a long technical video or customers don't care about it looking nice up close then a creator video won't help much. We'll let you know before you pay.

### Can we request certain features to be shown?

Yes! We'll include these in your brief. They will be added to your script and shot list. You'll approve these.

### Will we receive raw footage?

Yes! We'll include this if requested. Just mention this in your brief.

### Can we use these on our Shopify product page?

Yes! They're regular MP4s so you can upload them.

### How many samples should we send?

Typically one per creator. If your product runs out whilst filming then send an extra.

### How long will it take?

Usually 7-12 working days from brief to final files. If samples arrive late or we have slow feedback this can be longer.
