OVERVIEW

Why Brands Use AI UGC Video Creation

People ignore ads that look like TV commercials. A video that feels like it came from another person builds trust, and that’s what turns people scrolling their feed into customers.

When you run a lot of paid social, tired creative is the biggest headache. A normal shoot and edit for one new idea takes weeks. With AI-generated UGC video, you can get dozens of versions of the hook, the main part and the call to action in a few days.

We pair AI presenters with real footage of your product. The videos look believable and fit each platform, so you can try ideas fast, translate them into other languages and do creative testing at scale.
