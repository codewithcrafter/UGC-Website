# Case Study: D2C Skincare Brand Scales 3.8x ROAS with Creator UGC

> How MY UGC VIDEO engineered 15 creator-led TikTok & Meta ad creatives to reduce Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) by 42% in 60 days.

## Executive Summary

- **Client Category:** D2C Clean Beauty & Skincare
- **Campaign Objective:** Scale top-of-funnel customer acquisition on Instagram Reels and TikTok.
- **Key Result:** 3.8x Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) and a 42% reduction in blended CAC within 8 weeks.

## The Challenge

The brand had historically relied on polished studio product photography and high-end commercial videography. While visually aesthetic, their paid advertising metrics had stagnated:
- High Cost Per Click (CPC) above ₹45 ($0.55).
- Low 3-second hook retention (< 18%).
- Feed fatigue resulting in steep ad performance decay within 7 days.

## Creative Strategy & Execution

MY UGC VIDEO implemented a 15-asset creator sprint targeting three psychological triggers:
1. **The Skeptical First-Impression:** Creators testing the product over 7 consecutive days, demonstrating genuine skin texture changes.
2. **The "Stop Wasting Money" Comparison:** Direct side-by-side demonstration comparing the brand's active formulation against traditional generic creams.
3. **The Morning Routine Integration:** Seamless day-in-the-life placement showcasing quick application during busy schedules.

## Campaign Results

| **Click-Through Rate (CTR)** | 0.82% | 2.45% | +198% |
| **Blended CAC** | ₹890 | ₹516 | -42% |
| **Campaign ROAS** | 1.6x | 3.8x | +137% |
