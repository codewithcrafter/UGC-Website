Overview

Why Unboxing Videos Make People Want to Buy

Ads that look like TV commercials get skipped. A video that feels like it came from a real person gets watched, and that's often when someone stops scrolling and starts thinking about buying.

Opening the box is the first time a customer actually touches your brand. Online, nobody can walk into a store, so watching somebody else open a nicely packed order is the next best thing.

As a UGC unboxing video agency, we follow the whole moment: cutting the tape, lifting the lid, finding what's inside, feeling the materials and reacting on the spot. Buyers see that the packaging is good quality, and they want the same parcel on their doorstep. That's why unboxing videos for e-commerce brands keep turning up in feeds and on product pages.

Sound and ASMR

Clear audio of the box opening, paper crinkling, magnetic closures and everything else you'd hear while it's being unwrapped. ASMR unboxing videos depend on it.
