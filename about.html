OUR VIEW
HOW WE WORK

How a Project Runs

Same steps every time, so nobody has to guess what happens next.

01 BRIEF

Send Us the Brief

Tell us about the product, who it's for and where the videos will run. A rough brief is fine. We'll ask questions.

02 MATCH

We Pick Creators

We shortlist creators who suit the product and the audience.

03 PLAN

Script and Hooks

We work out the ideas and the first few seconds with you, and decide what viewers should do at the end.

04 SHOOT

Creators Film

They shoot at home or on location: an unboxing, a demo, a quick review.

05 EDIT

Edit and Check

We cut the video, add captions, fix the sound and watch every one before you do.

06 DELIVER

You Get the Files

Finished videos in the format you asked for, with ad usage rights.
