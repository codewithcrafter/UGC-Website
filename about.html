OUR MISSION

MAKING BRAND STORIES FEEL MORE HUMAN.

“We exist to close the widening gap between traditional corporate advertising and the genuine, relatable storytelling modern consumers actually trust.”

MY UGC VIDEO was founded on a simple observation: modern consumers no longer believe glossy corporate commercials or hyper-produced celebrity endorsements. When deciding what to buy, wear, or experience, people look to real peers—everyday creators who use products in their daily lives, share honest reactions, and speak without pretense.

However, for brands seeking to harness authentic content at scale, the execution gap has traditionally been immense. Direct creator outreach often means chasing unvetted influencers, dealing with inconsistent lighting and muffled audio, missing campaign deadlines, and navigating tangled usage rights. Conversely, traditional creative agencies charge exorbitant retainers while stripping away the very organic intimacy that makes creator content stop feeds.

Our mission is to eliminate that friction completely. We provide the infrastructure that empowers brands to tell relatable, human stories while simultaneously opening doors for creators to access meaningful, equitable commercial partnerships.

OUR COLLABORATION PROCESS

ONE CONNECTION. TWO CREATIVE WORLDS.

A structured, disciplined production journey that aligns brand goals with creator imagination—delivering consistent, high-converting video assets from initial brief to final delivery.

01 BRIEFING

Brand Briefing

Brands share their requirements, objectives, target audience and content expectations with our creative directors to establish clear campaign alignment.

02 MATCHING

Creator Matching

Suitable creators are identified according to the brand's requirements and content needs, ensuring demographic resonance and authentic on-camera delivery.

03 STRATEGY

Creative Planning

Concepts, content direction and scripts are planned according to the project, integrating tested hook angles, key benefits, and strong calls-to-action.

04 PRODUCTION

Content Production

Creators produce content based on the agreed creative direction, filming authentic lifestyle footage, unboxings, and hands-on demonstrations.

05 EDITING & QA

Editing & Quality Check

Content goes through editing and quality review, ensuring sharp pacing, dynamic mobile subtitles, audio enhancement, and narrative clarity.

06 DELIVERY

Final Delivery

The completed content is delivered according to the agreed project requirements, packaged with comprehensive digital advertising usage rights.
