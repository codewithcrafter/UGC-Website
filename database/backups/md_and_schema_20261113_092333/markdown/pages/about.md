## Who We Are

MY UGC VIDEO is a specialized creative studio and production network dedicated entirely to high-performing User Generated Content (UGC). We bridge the gap between creative storytelling and direct-response performance marketing.

### Our Mission
To replace generic, high-cost brand commercials with authentic, creator-led videos that build genuine buyer trust, stop the feed scroll, and dramatically lower customer acquisition costs (CAC) across paid social channels.

### What Sets Us Apart
- **Vetted Creator Network:** 1,500+ diverse, on-camera creators across Tier 1, 2, and 3 demographics in India.
- **Data-Driven Hook Strategy:** Over 250+ tested hook frameworks tailored to Meta (Instagram/Facebook) and YouTube Shorts.
- **End-to-End Production:** From creative scripting and product delivery to professional color grading, native sound design, and subtitle typography.
- **Full Commercial Licensing:** Complete perpetual usage rights for paid media campaigns without hidden royalty fees.
