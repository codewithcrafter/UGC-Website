UGC Product Video

Customers cannot touch your product through the screen, so they will zoom into the images and read the reviews. A UGC Product Video helps with this. A real creator will hold your product and demonstrate it so your customer can see the true size, texture and finish of your product.

Overview

Why People Trust Videos

Images are staged and everyone knows this, including your customers. By showing a regular person using your product at their own kitchen counter, it feels more authentic.

This is what our UGC product videos are based on. A real person that looks and sounds like your customer. No shiny background and no script. We think this is the whole point of being a UGC Product Video Agency.

Imagine a face serum being applied to the back of their hand or a blender lid locking into place. In just a few seconds it answers all your questions that your product page can't. This is why UGC video for e-commerce has become popular. [ADD: one genuine example from a previous project]

Close Ups

Will it be thick or runny? Glossy or matte? A texture video will help answer this in just a couple of seconds.

Filmed At Home

We film with our creators at home using natural lighting. It doesn't need to be perfect!

Video Styles

3 Styles We Create Most

Not all your customers will be at the same stage in their purchase journey. These 3 UGC product videos will cover the typical stages.

Direct Response

Step-by-Step

Your creator will show how they use the product, how much and how they apply. Perfect for customers who are new to your brand.

Best For: New Customers 15-30 seconds
Demonstration

Close Up Texture

The camera will be close to the formula showing it being spread, absorbed or firm. Natural sound is kept. Perfect for demonstrating the product.

Best For: Product Demonstration 20-35 seconds
Social Proof

First Impression

Your creator will give an honest reaction to using the product for the first time. This is never scripted. Perfect for retargeting.

Best For: Retargeting 30-45 seconds
